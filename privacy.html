Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

What we collect

  • Account: email address, username and password (stored encrypted, never readable).
  • What you add: photos, videos, item names, sizes, stores, tags and albums.
  • Social: friends, borrow requests, chat messages, saved items, blocks and reports.
  • Device: a push notification token, if you allow notifications.

Why

Only to run the app: show your wardrobe, share it with friends, handle borrowing and chat, send notifications, reset passwords and keep the community safe. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and your data is never sold.

Who can see your data

Everything is private until you are friends. Friends only see the albums you share with them. Any signed-in user can find your username in search and see your profile picture, so people can tell accounts apart. Saved items and blocks are only visible to you. People you block cannot find or see you.

Services we use

  • Supabase: database, file storage and login.
  • Expo, Apple and Google: delivering push notifications.
  • Google (Gmail): sending account emails such as password reset codes.
  • Discord: when you file a report, its details are sent to a private channel that only the developer can see, so it can be reviewed quickly.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, photos, videos, albums, items, friends, loans, chats and saved items. Reports you made or that concern you may be kept to keep the community safe.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how it is used. You can delete everything yourself in Settings. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).
